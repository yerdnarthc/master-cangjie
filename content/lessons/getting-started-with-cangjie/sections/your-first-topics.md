You have met the main tools. In **Installing the Cangjie Toolchain**, you will prepare them on your computer. Then **Running the First Cangjie Program** walks you through a greeting program, from its source file to the output you can check.

You can also practice in the MasterCangjie browser editor without installing anything locally. Your code runs on the server using Cangjie 1.0.5. Learning the local steps gives you another place to practice when you want to write programs outside the website.
