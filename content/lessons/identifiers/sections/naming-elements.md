In Cangjie, you give names to variables, functions, types, and packages. These names are called identifiers. An identifier connects a clear, human-readable name to a value, variable, or definition in your program.

Good identifiers tell you at a glance what a value represents. For example, `totalScore` or `calculateTax` immediately communicates purpose, whereas cryptic names like `x1` or `temp2` create confusion as programs grow.
